## TRACK 7 — OBBBA Spaceport Facility Bonds (NEW — July 1, 2026)

### What changed
The **One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA)** — signed into law July 4, 2025 — created **tax-exempt private activity bonds for spaceport facilities**. As of June 2026 this provision is fully operative and now recognized as a transformational financing mechanism for space infrastructure (SpaceNews, June 1, 2026).

### Why this matters for Exos NCE
- **Montana Spaceport**: the bond provision allows Montana Spaceport & Test Range to issue **municipal bonds** to finance the BLK3 manufacturing/launch facility — at lower cost of capital, longer maturities, and **no project size cap**. Exos as FAA-licensed anchor tenant/operator is exactly the "durable commercial commitment" (offtake / capacity reservation) that credit-enhances the bond for muni investors.
- **New Mexico (Spaceport America)**: same authority applies. Exos's existing tenant relationship + long-term launch service agreements = bond credit anchor.
- **Manufacturing nexus**: facilities "adjacent to or with nexus to a licensed launch/reentry site" are eligible — this includes the BLK3 build line if co-located at or near either spaceport.
- **100% private use permitted**: no compliance distortion; Exos operates the facility, state issues the bonds.

### How to use this
This is a **new financing layer** that sits ON TOP of the existing econ-dev grant tracks — bonds finance long-lived infrastructure (hangars, integration facility, launch pads) while grants/LEDA fund job-creation milestones. Edgar's congressional deck already asks for 51 USC Ch. 511 Space Transportation Infrastructure Matching Grants authority; add OBBBA bonds as the parallel capital mechanism.
