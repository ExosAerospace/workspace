## 1. Bottom line up front

A **kill vehicle (KV)** — the hit-to-kill interceptor front end — is defined by four subsystems Exos and its consortium **do not have and cannot credibly stand up**: the **seeker**, the **divert & attitude control system (DACS)**, **missile-grade GNC / flight software**, and **weapons-system integration + past performance**. So Exos **cannot prime or fulfill a low-cost KV**, and no combination of our current partners closes that gap.

What the Exos-led consortium *can* credibly deliver is the **affordable enabling layer around** the KV: low-cost **target vehicles**, high-cadence **recoverable flight-test / risk-reduction**, a domestic **high-temp materials + composite-structures sub-tier** for the KV itself, minimal-infrastructure mobile test ops, and near-zero-cost flight nav. That is a **sub-tier / teammate** role under a KV prime — **not** a prime bid.

**Therefore the play is the fallback by design: approach a KV prime and plug in underneath it. Lockheed Martin is both our closest relationship and a genuine KV/interceptor prime (PAC-3 MSE, THAAD, NGI). It is the right first door.**

## 2. What a low-cost kill vehicle actually requires (the requirement set)

| KV core subsystem | What it is | Who owns this class of work |
|---|---|---|
| **Seeker** | IR/imaging (or RF) terminal-homing sensor + optics + focal-plane | RTX, L3Harris, specialized seeker houses |
| **DACS** | Fast-response divert & attitude thrusters for terminal maneuver | Aerojet/L3H, RTX |
| **GNC + flight computer** | Missile-grade, rad-tolerant, real-time guidance/nav/control | Prime + missile-electronics tier |
| **Discrimination / aimpoint algorithms** | Onboard signal processing, target discrimination | Prime + FFRDC/labs |
| **Structures & thermal** | Lightweight, aeroheating/exo-atmospheric-tolerant KV body, nozzle, throat | **Materials & composites tier ← Exos consortium plays here** |
| **Boost / propulsion stack** | Solid-rocket booster to deliver KV to engagement | Prime + solid-motor houses (Aerojet, Northrop) |
| **Weapon-system integration + past performance** | Whole-interceptor integration, flight qual, MDA past performance | **Prime (LM / RTX / Boeing / Northrop)** |

The three irreducible, KV-defining elements — **seeker, DACS, weapons integration/past-performance** — are exactly where we have nothing. That is not a gap we can buy down with a teaming agreement; it is the core of a different company's business.

## 4. Fallback plan — approach the prime (Lockheed Martin)

Since there is no consortium-prime fit, the KV lane is captured **as a sub under a prime**. LM is the right first door on two counts: it is our **closest relationship**, and it is an actual **KV/interceptor prime** — **PAC-3 / PAC-3 MSE, THAAD, and the Next Generation Interceptor (NGI)**, all hit-to-kill.

**Geography advantage:** LM's KV/interceptor house is **Missiles & Fire Control (MFC)** in **Grand Prairie, TX (Dallas metro)** — roughly an hour from Greenville. A local, minimal-infrastructure, ITAR/CMMC-ready test partner is a natural regional fit.

### Warm paths we already hold
- **Joseph Epstein** — Lockheed Martin, Transfer-of-Innovation lead (`joseph.epstein@lmco.com`) — **active contact, last spoken 5/8/26** (Lizi follow-up logged 6/17). Warmest programmatic entry; route to LM MFC.
- **Chris Moran** — VP/GM, **LM Ventures** (made the Venus Aerospace RDRE investment; fund expanded to $1B, Apr 2026). CVC/propulsion angle; existing draft `Outreach_Lockheed_Martin_Chris_Moran.docx`. Secondary path (investment/RDRE, not the KV program office).

### What Exos + EIC offers LM on a low-cost KV program (our three FFP-ownable scopes)
1. **Affordable target & recoverable test-bed** — reusable, minimal-infrastructure suborbital platform to fly KV seekers/DACS/components for high-cadence risk reduction and to serve as low-cost **targets** for interceptor testing. (Exos + Starfighters; backup: Exos self-perform.)
2. **Domestic high-temp materials + composite-structures sub-tier** for the KV body/nozzle/thermal — MATECH CMC + Super Carbon C-C + SWC/Hybron/CDT structures. Directly supports LM's "low-cost" + domestic-supply and second-source goals. (Backup: 2-deep on structures across SWC/Hybron/CDT.)
3. **Cost/schedule + local ITAR/CMMC-ready ops** — commercial cost model, TX-local to LM MFC, compliance boundary via Atomus, near-zero-cost flight nav (GEO-FOG spares). The "low-cost, non-traditional, made-in-Texas" narrative MDA is explicitly funding.

### The ask to LM (positioning, not a prime bid)
> *"Exos is not pitching to build your kill vehicle — you own that. We're offering LM MFC an affordable, local, reusable target/test-bed to buy down KV risk at high cadence, plus a domestic high-temp materials and composite-structures sub-tier for the KV itself. We de-risk your low-cost interceptor cost curve; you keep the weapon system."*

### If LM says no — the prime bench (in order)
1. **Lockheed Martin (MFC)** — closest relationship, local, PAC-3/THAAD/NGI. *(Primary.)*
2. **RTX (Raytheon Missiles & Defense)** — EKV, SM-3, GPI; largest KV franchise. Colder relationship.
3. **Northrop Grumman** — NGI competitor heritage, solid motors, targets.
4. **Boeing (GMD prime)** — GMD/EKV integration.
5. **MACH-TB 2.0 route (Kratos-led)** — not a KV prime, but the affordable-flight-test/target on-ramp already identified in the base brief; parallel path for the target/test-services scope specifically.
