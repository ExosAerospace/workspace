PART 1 — THE SLIDE: "Built to Compound: Exos Runs as an AI-Native Company"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Top line (thesis tie-in):**
The next industrial era is the build-out of the physical world — a ~$20T re-industrialization where the winners pair physical capability with intelligence density. Exos is one of only three FAA-licensed reusable rocket launch providers in the United States — and it is run as an AI-native firm. The same discipline that makes a reusable rocket cheap to fly makes the *company* cheap to run.

**Three operating advantages (each real and in place today):**

**1. Capital-efficient by design.**
A small human core directs a governed fleet of AI agents across capital formation, BD, research, and back-office. Cost scales with compute — tokens as a cost of goods — rather than headcount. Practical effect: more milestones per dollar, lower burn per unit of progress. Capital is deployed against capability (Scorpius ROFR, RDRE flight-test path, BLK4), not org overhead.

**2. Governed autonomy — de-risked, not hand-wavy.**
Exos operates a four-pillar governance layer on its agents: trusted evaluations with drift thresholds, searchable audit logs with correlation IDs, granular rollback on every action, and a human-in-the-loop approval queue for anything irreversible or external. Every autonomous action is logged, attributable, and reversible. Our eval runner currently reports a **23% human-override rate across 114 governed decisions, overall GREEN against a 40% ceiling** — the framework's own Quiet-Drift measure, computed live. Most "AI-native" startups have the agents but not the brakes.

**3. Compliance-native intelligence.**
Inference runs on in-house, ITAR/CUI-safe infrastructure across more than one model family — no single-vendor lock-in, no export-control exposure from routing controlled data to a public API. The resulting decision-and-audit trail becomes a compounding proprietary asset: the company's own operating knowledge, captured as data it owns.

**Why it matters to this round:**
Intelligence density is the multiplier on every dollar raised. It lowers the capital required to reach each milestone, and the operating model itself — governed, compliant, owned — is defensible. You are funding a reusable-launch platform run by an organization engineered to do more with less.

**Footer (honesty line):** Operating model instantiated in 2026 and in active build-out; presented as execution capability, not a claim of full autonomy.

because the vehicle returns and flies again.
