## How to Detect a New RLV License

The FAA announces all new launch licenses via:
- FAA Newsroom: faa.gov/newsroom
- Federal Register notices
- Industry press coverage (SpaceNews, Ars Technica, etc.)
- The Dynamic Regulatory System: drs.faa.gov

Search terms: "FAA" + "reusable launch vehicle" + "license" OR "Part 450" + "reusable" + new company names (Stoke Space, Phantom Space, etc.)
