## The Fraud — How It Worked

1. **Approach:** Jung + Singh + Nemani approached Exos via brokers (Bornmueller/Gehrmann) offering $315M development financing, claiming syndicate loan "with Dubai Holding"
2. **False credibility:** Aras website claimed 200M AED in funded loans — no evidence of actual loans; fake sheikh presented at contract signing; forged Emirates Islamic Bank statement presented as proof of funds
3. **Contract signed:** Nov 2021 — $449,279.00 contract for development finance for Exos projects
4. **Payments made:** $613,097.63 total wired to Emirates Islamic Bank account connected to Jung/ARAS
5. **Immediate diversion:** Funds transferred out of the account the day after receipt — not applied to contracted services
6. **Continued extraction:** Over €1,500,000 removed from same account (2020–2022), reducing it to near zero; total victims paid >€1,600,000 into that one account
7. **Cover mechanism:** Jung appointed Dogan as PoA to claim personal ignorance of fund movements (same playbook used in German courts)
8. **Concealment:** Jung used fake name "Frank Kahn" throughout; prior German criminal conviction for financing fraud concealed; Aras banned by German finance authority from advertising loans

**⚠️ ARABIC vs ENGLISH VERSION DISCREPANCY — POTENTIAL FRAUD INDICATOR**

The Arabic-language version of the same contract (D211006) contains different financial terms than the English version:

| Term | English Version | Arabic Version |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Commitment interest rate | **1.512%** p.a. | **1.50%** p.a. |
| Fixed charge | **3.84%** p.a. | **3.78%** p.a. |

Different financial terms in two versions of the same contract is a classic fraud technique — present whichever version is more favorable in any given dispute, or use the discrepancy to create confusion and delay. This should be explicitly raised with Dubai Police and any legal proceedings.
