EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Exos Aerospace Systems & Technologies, Inc. ("Exos"), a small business and
nontraditional defense contractor, requests membership in the AEDC Velocity
Alliance to bring the Alliance a capability its ground-test enterprise does
not have on its own: a reusable, recoverable flight-test range that extends
AEDC ground test into real flight. On the BLK3 Precision Suborbital Reusable
Launch Vehicle, one of only three U.S.-licensed reusable suborbital systems,
Alliance members can validate materials, thermal protection, structures, and
subsystems in the actual hypersonic-adjacent reentry, microgravity,
dynamic-load, and thermal-vacuum environments a ground facility cannot fully
reproduce, and recover the hardware intact for post-flight analysis, closing
the ground-to-flight test loop.

That flight-test range is backed by deep supporting engineering: flight-proven,
lunar-heritage Type 5 cryogenic and high-pressure composite systems (through
internal affiliate Scorpius Space Launch Company) and advanced propulsion
engineering, directly applicable to AEDC cryogenic and propulsion test-stand
modernization. Exos leads a capability team that adds above-Mach-2 airborne
captive-carry test through external teaming partner Starfighters International
Inc., and offers the Alliance, at no cost, bonus in-space propellant transfer
flight research that also advances an applied-for NASA orbital experiment. This
whitepaper responds to Section 4.0, Parts 1 through 4.

2.1  Project Type 12 — Mechanical Engineering Services
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    PROBLEM. Lunar-lander propulsion required lightweight cryogenic tanks to
    contain liquid oxygen and liquid methane through launch loads, deep-space
    thermal cycling, and lunar surface exposure, where metallic tanks imposed
    prohibitive mass and conventional composite tanks microcracked at cryogenic
    temperature.

    ROLE AND SCOPE. Exos, through affiliate Scorpius, designed, analyzed, and
    manufactured the Type 5 (linerless) all-composite cryogenic tank. Work
    combined structural finite element analysis, transient thermal analysis,
    and laminate permeability modeling, with a room-temperature-cure resin
    system formulated to reduce the coefficient-of-thermal-expansion mismatch
    that drives cryogenic microcracking.

    QUALITY AND VALIDATION. Progressive qualification ran from coupon cryogenic
    thermal cycling and helium leak testing, through sub-scale leak-before-burst
    and full-scale proof, vibration, and combined-environment testing. The tanks
    then flew as fuel and LOX tanks on the Intuitive Machines Odysseus and
    Athena lunar landers, containing cryogenic propellants through launch,
    transit, descent, and surface operations. This is believed to be the only
    TRL 9 linerless composite cryogenic tank with demonstrated lunar heritage.

2.2  Project Type 4 — High Pressure Process Systems (> 150 PSIG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    ROLE AND SCOPE. The Type 5 cryogenic tanks are flight-qualified composite
    pressure vessels operating well above 150 PSIG. Exos and Scorpius performed
    the pressure-system design, boss and joint interface engineering, and
    qualification testing, including proof-pressure testing and cryogenic helium
    leak-rate verification against mission-duration containment thresholds. This
    competency transfers directly to cryogenic and high-pressure test-stand and
    propellant-system engineering for AEDC facility modernization.

2.3  Project Type 5 — Hazardous Process Systems / Propulsion Flight Test
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    ROLE AND SCOPE. Exos designs, integrates, and operates hazardous cryogenic
    propellant systems (liquid oxygen and liquid methane) and conducts
    integration and reusable suborbital flight test of government-furnished
    propulsion and test articles on the BLK3 platform, one of only three
    U.S.-licensed reusable suborbital systems. Exos owns the safety design,
    hazard analysis, and range-safety engineering for cryogenic and energetic
    media, providing a flight-validated complement to ground test. This project
    type is the basis for the partnership described in Part 4.

4.3  Innovation Differentiator — No-Cost In-Space Propellant Transfer Research
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Beyond the partnership above, Exos offers the Alliance an innovation
    differentiator at no additional cost to the Government: bonus in-space
    propellant transfer flight research carried on BLK3 suborbital test
    missions.

    OFFER. On Alliance flight-test missions, Exos can fly, as a no-cost bonus
    payload, a tank-to-tank cryogenic propellant transfer experiment built on
    the flight-proven Type 5 tankage described in Section 2.1. The experiment
    exercises cryogenic fluid management and propellant transfer under the
    microgravity, boost, and recovery environments of a reusable suborbital
    flight, with hardware and data recovered intact for post-flight analysis —
    data a ground stand cannot fully replicate.

    VALUE TO AEDC. The resulting cryogenic fluid management and propellant
    transfer data set is directly applicable to AEDC's cryogenic and propulsion
    test-stand modernization (Sections 2.2 and 2.3), giving the Alliance a
    recurring, no-cost in-space cryogenic test capability alongside its ground
    test portfolio.

    CROSS-AGENCY LEVERAGE. The same experiment de-risks and feeds Exos's
    applied-for NASA orbital flight experiment under the NASA TechLeap
    Robotically Manipulated Payload Challenge (RMPC), establishing a
    ground-to-suborbital-to-orbital propellant transfer test continuum spanning
    the Air Force test enterprise and NASA. The Alliance therefore receives
    bonus cryogenic in-space research that also advances a separately competed
    national program, at no charge to the Government.
