PROPOSED SOLUTION

Exos Aerospace is prepared to commence development of a proprietary Rotating Detonation Rocket Engine under a separate Other Transaction Authority (OTA) prototype agreement, providing a viable alternative propulsion solution for the HVRR test vehicle should GFE delivery timelines continue to be affected.

We propose pursuing this through the Space Enterprise Consortium (SpEC) / NSTXL OTA vehicle or the AFWERX SEC vehicle — whichever AFTC finds most expedient. This would be a new, separate instrument and would not modify or disrupt FA930025C6022.

WHY EXOS IS UNIQUELY POSITIONED TO EXECUTE

Exos Aerospace has spent over a decade developing rocket engines. Our team has designed, built, and tested hundreds of rocket engines across multiple platforms and propellant combinations. This is not a new capability for us — it is our core competency.

What makes this moment different is our ability to combine that engineering heritage with two additional assets:

1. NASA Technical Data — We hold a NASA Data Usage Agreement granting access to NASA Marshall Space Flight Center's RDRE technical data from the INRODES and Early Career Initiative programs, including drawings, models, hot fire test data, design correlations, best practices, and lessons learned. This gives us a validated technical baseline that compresses years of development time.

2. AI-Accelerated Design — Exos is actively leveraging advanced AI capabilities to accelerate flight-weight RDRE design iteration. By combining our propulsion engineering heritage with NASA's empirical RDRE dataset and modern AI-assisted design tools, we believe we can rapidly advance flight-weight RDRE designs that bring genuine new propulsion capabilities to the warfighter — at a pace and cost not previously achievable.

We operate the BLK3 reusable suborbital launch vehicle (135 kg / 100 km altitude), one of three FAA-licensed reusable suborbital RLV platforms in the U.S., which serves as the intended flight demonstration platform. ITAR compliance is maintained throughout — all NASA DUA data is held within U.S. persons only.
