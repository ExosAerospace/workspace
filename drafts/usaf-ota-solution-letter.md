**Proposed Solution**

Exos Aerospace is prepared to commence development of a proprietary RDRE under a separate Other Transaction Authority (OTA) prototype agreement, structured to provide a viable alternative propulsion solution for the HVRR test vehicle should GFE delivery timelines continue to be affected.

We propose pursuing this through the **Space Enterprise Consortium (SEC) / AFWERX OTA vehicle**, which is well-suited to rapid prototype development of this nature and is already established within the Air Force ecosystem. This would be a new, separate instrument — not a modification to FA930025C6022 — allowing both efforts to proceed in parallel without disrupting the current contract structure.

**Why Exos is Positioned to Execute**

Exos Aerospace has an active technical foundation for RDRE development:

- We hold a **NASA Data Usage Agreement** granting access to NASA Marshall Space Flight Center's RDRE technical data, including drawings, models, hot fire test data, design best practices, and lessons learned from the INRODES/ECI RDRE programs — providing a proven technical baseline
- Our engineering team has the propulsion development capability to apply this data toward a vehicle-ready RDRE
- We operate the **BLK3** reusable suborbital launch vehicle (135 kg / 100 km), one of three FAA-licensed reusable suborbital RLV platforms in the U.S., which would serve as the flight demonstration platform
- ITAR compliance is maintained — all NASA DUA data is held within U.S. persons only

**What We Are Requesting**

1. A brief meeting with you and your technical team to discuss the GFE schedule outlook and align on whether a parallel OTA development path makes sense for the program
2. Guidance on whether to approach this through the SEC/AFWERX vehicle or an alternative OTA mechanism preferred by AFTC
3. If appropriate, an introduction to the relevant AFWERX program office or SEC consortium manager to initiate the OTA process

We are committed to mission success on the HVRR program and want to ensure the Air Force has options to maintain schedule regardless of GFE outcome. We are ready to move quickly.
