# Memorandum of Understanding — DRAFT for review
## Exos × UT Dallas "EXO3 Payload Capstone" (Fall 2026)

**Parties:** Exos Aerospace Systems & Technologies, Inc. ("Exos") and The University of Texas at Dallas ("UTD").
**Exos lead:** Lizi Quinn (relationship owner + public-content approver); John Quinn, Cofounder & CEO (sponsor, supportive).
**UTD lead:** Edwin Van Der Vlist (capstone program administrator).
**Status:** DRAFT — for internal review; route to counsel before signature.

---

### 1. Purpose
UTD provides a Fall 2026 marketing capstone team to act as Exos's agency of record, marketing and filling the open ~135 kg on the BLK3 Flight 2 payload bay (early 2027, Spaceport America, NM) under the SPACEavailable…™ campaign — directing Exos's EXO3 AI agent fleet. Students are graded on real payload outcomes, not effort.

### 2. Roles
- **Lizi Quinn (Exos)** — owns the UTD relationship and is the approval gate for anything that goes public (media, website, outreach naming Exos).
- **John Quinn (Exos)** — sponsor; supportive; pulled in only for critical-impact decisions.
- **Exo (EXO3 agent)** — the operating engine; gates student design/build requests, generates code/creative in an isolated sandbox, and runs the scorecard.
- **Edwin Van Der Vlist (UTD)** — program administrator; uploads the roster; parallel rights with Jon + Lizi to see/manage student accounts; joint determiner (with Exos) of material contribution.

### 3. Student access & security
- Each student receives a **scoped Mission Control login** limited to the "Payload Sales" board + the progress tracker. No access to Exos CRM, financials, decisions, documents, or internal systems.
- Access is **monitored**: any attempt to exceed scope is logged and alerts Exos + Lizi.
- **Restricted from students:** Narravity and Napier Park (walled off).
- **Design-request loop:** students request builds; Exo generates in an isolated capstone sandbox (never Exos production); changes are reviewed and reversible; nothing executes on Exos systems.

### 4. Timeline (15 weeks; Edwin sets the start date)
- **Weeks 1–2:** onboard, team sets its own space-themed name, lock the 10× baseline, define ICP/segments; **Exos site visit** (Greenville, TX, 3–4 hrs — Jon/Lizi + BD team address the team + facility tour).
- **Week 3:** ⚡ **Challenge kickoff** — AI-only vs Hybrid both live.
- **Weeks 4–15:** campaign build, outreach at scale, weekly pipeline gates, handoffs, 100 kg push, final presentation + playbook + Payload Sales Engineer selections.
- A live weekly-gate tracker shows progress to the team and Edwin.

### 5. The Challenge (the spine)
A controlled A/B on matched prospect cohorts: **AI-only** (fleet autonomous) vs **Hybrid** (students directing the fleet), same tools and approval gate, metrics pre-registered. Tests whether human-directed AI beats autonomous AI on real revenue.

### 6. Grading & metrics (weekly, real dollars)
- **Team outcomes:** qualified pipeline $, high-prob opportunities vs the 10× target, kg advanced/booked, funnel conversion; plus **AI-effectiveness** (leverage, reply rate, attribution, cost/opportunity).
- **Individual score (role-fair, /100):** primary-track impact 35, contribution-to-team-outcome 20, AI-leverage 15, consistency 15, instructor+peer assessment 15. Four equal tracks — Growth/Outreach, Web & Pipeline Engineering, Creative/Content, Data/Analytics & Ops — each scored in its own currency; **specializing to the website/pipeline build (or creative, or analytics) is a full, unpenalized path.**

### 7. Individual accountability
It is a team effort, but **each person must materially contribute** to earn rewards. Material contribution = individual score ≥ 60 in their track **and** affirmed by **both Edwin and Exos**.

### 8. Reward ladder (two rungs; both gated per person)
- **75 kg booked → 1U payload reward** ($8,000 value; contents ≤ 1 kg): eligible members place a small item and **decide its use** — engraved dog tags/names, or **flown-item/sticker sales for a university-approved team activity** (their fundraiser, not Exos revenue), or both. All items flight-safe per the PUG, export-safe.
- **100 kg booked → launch trip** to the BLK3 launch (Spaceport America; est. April 2027): eligible members travel. Exos provides a 15-passenger van, ~4-day trip, group lodging (men/women separate), and a **Divvy virtual-card food budget** (actual receipted expenses up to $55/person/day, food-only merchant controls, students submit reports in the Divvy app — a real corporate-expense experience). **Every student is issued and physically handed a company Divvy card at the on-site kickoff** and holds it through to the launch as a briefing lesson in professional responsibility; cards remain inactive until the launch window is set and the student is booked (a non-eligible student's card simply never activates).
- **Perfectly clear:** anyone who does not materially participate goes on **no** trip and places **nothing** in the 1U.

### 9. Total Exos contribution
~**$16,100 in direct rewards** for a successful project ($8,000 1U spaceflight + ~$8,100 launch trip), plus substantial in-kind value (EXO3 platform + agent-fleet access, Exo's build time, brand assets/media, CEO + BD time) and a **contracted Payload Sales Engineer** pathway for top performers.

### 10. Payload Sales Engineer track (talent pipeline)
Top performers may earn a contracted Payload Sales Engineer position. Compensation = commission on the **customer payload bookings they source**, via Exos's commission system — not tied to any capital raise. A hired student receives **attribution credit carried forward** for capstone-sourced opportunities, but commissions are payable **only on future sales that close beyond the capstone**.

### 11. Intellectual property
Exos owns all capstone deliverables (site, copy, campaign assets, pipeline). UTD confirms its capstone terms do not conflict.

### 12. Media
UTD is responsible for local and national media and audience-building around the "AI challenged by UTD" story. **Any media naming Exos or its capabilities passes Exos (Lizi) review first** — positioning rules apply (flying since 2018; one of only three FAA-licensed reusable rocket launch providers in the U.S.; never "first commercial flight"; no overstatement; export-safe).

### 13. Sources & attribution
Primary source: the Exos BLK3 **Payload User's Guide**. Context: historical Exos + Armadillo Aerospace YouTube. **Any material used from Armadillo Aerospace must credit "Armadillo Aerospace"** (Exos is Armadillo's commercial successor).

### 14. Compliance
- **Student data / FERPA:** minimal collection; Edwin uploads from the UTD side.
- **Export diligence:** the roster captures US-citizen / non-US status as a documentation step, **not a disqualifier** — the student scope is public/commercial marketing data only (no ITAR/CUI); non-US students participate fully.
- **Securities:** any public investor/offering language is counsel-gated, must fit the applicable Reg D exemption's solicitation rules, and is authored by Exos + counsel only — **never by students**.
- **Hosting:** the SPACEavailable microsite is served at spaceavailable.exosaero.com (static, reviewed-before-deploy).

### 15. Term & signatures
Effective on signature; runs the Fall 2026 semester. Either party may terminate for cause with written notice; student data handled per FERPA on termination.
