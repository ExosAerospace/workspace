Hello John,

That membership paragraph works well, and pulling the active-member language straight from the Adaptive Structures charter is the right move, since it anchors us to an approved AIAA precedent rather than something we invented. I have adopted your paragraph verbatim as the base membership text in the charter.

I also agree with your sequencing, and I think it is the important point in the thread. Step up the committee's activity and give people real ways to contribute before anyone talks about striking names off the roster. Your subcommittee construct is the mechanism that does that. Market forecasting, industry engagement, and educational outreach each become a seat someone can form and chair, which is how a nominal member turns into an active one. I have written those three in as the seed subcommittee set, with reusable upper stages and operability/reliability/safety as two more the survey can confirm.

On the participation standard Linton raised, I have kept it in the charter but repositioned it exactly the way you framed it. It is an optional tool for the annual review, not a mid-cycle removal device, and it reads against the September 2027 recruiting window you pointed to rather than any quarterly re-vote. Anilkumar's three-year term and the uniform annual recruiting-and-appointment cycle are written into the governance section as the backbone, so the charter stays aligned with the standard AIAA process.

On the survey, I have a Round 1 first pass drafted so we react to something concrete rather than a blank form. Twelve questions, mostly ranking and multi-select, about ten minutes, built as a light two-round Delphi. Round 1 diverges and collects scope candidates and desired outcomes, Round 2 hands back a consolidated list to prioritize, and I bring a draft charter with an already-ranked scope to the next meeting instead of another open discussion. The questions map one to one onto the charter sections, and the membership question is framed opportunity-first to match your steer. I can stand it up in Forms and field it for a week whenever the group is comfortable with the questions.
