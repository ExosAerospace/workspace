**RE: Formal Notification of Government Furnished Equipment Delivery Risk — Contract [Contract Number] — HVRR / RDRE Test Vehicle**

Dear [CO Name],

I am writing to formally notify the Government of a potential schedule risk associated with the Government Furnished Equipment (GFE) Rotating Detonation Rocket Engine (RDRE) required under Contract [Contract Number] for the High Velocity Research Rocket (HVRR) test vehicle program.

**Nature of the Concern**

Exos Aerospace Systems & Technologies, Inc. has growing concern regarding the timely delivery of the GFE RDRE to support the HVRR program schedule. To date, we have not received confirmed delivery dates, hardware status updates, or integration documentation sufficient to support our planned test vehicle build and integration milestones. This uncertainty creates downstream risk to the overall program schedule and to Exos's ability to perform under the terms of the contract.

**Contractual Impact**

Per FAR 52.245-1 (Government Property) and applicable contract terms, Exos's performance obligations are predicated on timely receipt of GFE. Delays in GFE delivery constitute an excusable delay under the contract and may affect cost, schedule, and technical performance baselines. We are providing this formal written notification to establish the record and ensure the Government has visibility into this risk at the earliest opportunity.

**Request for Government Action**

Exos respectfully requests the following from the Contracting Officer within 15 business days of this letter:

1. **Confirmed GFE delivery date and current hardware status** for the RDRE to be furnished under this contract
2. **Identification of the GFE source and any known technical or schedule risks** affecting delivery
3. **Authorization to identify and evaluate alternative RDRE sources** at Government expense, in the event GFE delivery is delayed beyond [insert critical milestone date], to ensure HVRR program objectives are met without interruption

**Proposed Path Forward**

Exos is not raising this issue to create adversarial friction — we are raising it because we are committed to mission success and want to get ahead of a risk before it becomes a problem. We have significant in-house RDRE technical capability, including development work conducted under an active NASA Data Usage Agreement utilizing NASA Marshall Space Flight Center technical data. If authorized and funded, Exos has a credible path to develop or source an alternative RDRE to serve as backup or primary propulsion for the HVRR, maintaining program schedule and government objectives.

We request a meeting at your earliest convenience to discuss this matter and align on the best path forward.
