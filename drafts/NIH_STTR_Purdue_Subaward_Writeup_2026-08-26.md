## 1. Purdue's role in one paragraph
Under the Exos–UW NIH STTR (PA-27-102, September 8, 2026), Purdue joins by **subaward (≤30% of work)** providing suborbital experiment design and methods, led by **Prof. Steven Collicott (School of Aeronautics & Astronautics)** — a committed Exos partner (SACSS letter of support in hand, "onboard" per his 7/27 note). **Prof. Estelle (Sunghee) Park (Weldon School of Biomedical Engineering)** is welcomed as an **optional co-investigator** strengthening the biomedical bench. Dr. Edward Kelly (UW) is the biomedical science lead and research institution of record; Exos is the small business concern and prime applicant.

## 2. Why Purdue on the subaward
- **Collicott (AAE)** — suborbital microgravity experiment design and methods; already an Exos partner, so this is an execution role, not a cold start.
- **Park (BME, optional)** — stem-cell-derived organoids-on-chip and organ-on-chip systems (trained under Dan Huh, UPenn); adds biomedical depth to the onset-kinetics aim if she elects to join.
- **Same institution** → one Purdue Sponsored Programs Services subaward routing, no cross-department friction.

## 3. Subaward scope
- **Suborbital experiment methods & payload experiment design [Collicott]** — flight-profile experiment design, microgravity-onset measurement approach, payload-experiment methods feeding the Exos platform and the UW science.
- **Optional organoid/MPS co-investigation [Park]** — organoid/organ-on-chip contribution to Aim 2 (onset-kinetics characterization), complementary to Dr. Kelly's kidney-chip MPS.

## 4. Where the subaward sits in the specific aims
1. Platform integration & validation — *Exos lead.*
2. Onset-kinetics characterization — *Kelly / UW lead; Collicott suborbital methods; Park organoid co-investigation (optional).*
3. Screening-pipeline demonstration — *joint.*

## 5. Timeline & the binding gate
- **Purdue SPS subaward routing** — start now; **~Sep 1** internal-lock target (~5 business days before deadline).
- **Sep 8, 2026** — STTR PA-27-102 deadline. UW OSP and Purdue SPS route in parallel; the later governs.
