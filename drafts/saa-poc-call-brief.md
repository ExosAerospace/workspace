# NASA SAA POC Call Brief
_Prep for call with Exos's NASA technical counterpart on the RDRE Space Act Agreement_
_Created: 2026-03-25_

---

## Objective

One call, two outcomes:
1. **TechPort** — confirm RDRE program listed with Exos named as industry partner
2. **Flight demo path** — identify what funded milestone/contract vehicle could support a BLK3 flight demo of RDRE hardware

---

## Background to Know Before the Call

- NASA MSFC RDRE lead: **Thomas Teasley** (combustion devices engineer)
- INRODES Phase 1 hardware being fabricated by AME (~$248K, delivery ~June 2025)
- NASA RDRE tested successfully Dec 2023 — 251 seconds, 5,800 lbs thrust
- Partners on prior tests: In Space LLC, Purdue University, Venus Aerospace, Glenn Research Center
- NASA RDRE is managed under the **Game Changing Development Program** within STMD
- NASA 2026 SBIR is transitioning to a **Broad Agency Announcement (BAA)** format — release TBD

## Questions to Ask

### On TechPort:
1. Is the Exos RDRE program currently listed in NASA TechPort?
2. Is Exos named as the industry partner on the entry?
3. If not listed — can you add it, or who do we contact to get it added?
4. What description/TRL level should we use for the current Exos RDRE program status?

### On Flight Demo Path:
1. As INRODES hardware matures, what's the anticipated path to a flight demonstration?
2. Is there a funded milestone in the current SAA or program plan for a suborbital flight test?
3. Would BLK3 (135 kg / 100 km) be the right vehicle class for INRODES flight demo hardware?
4. What contract vehicle would NASA use to fund a flight demo — task order under existing SAA, new contract, SBIR Phase III, or something else?
5. Who else at MSFC or STMD should we be talking to about positioning BLK3 for this?

### On SBIR Phase III:
1. Does the RDRE work under the SAA create a SBIR Phase III sole-source pathway for Exos?
2. Has any of the underlying RDRE technology been funded through NASA SBIR/STTR?
