## THE COMPANY

**EXOS is reusable flight test infrastructure** for the defense and space industrial base.

Customers fly **subsystems, not missions.**

EXOS enables:
- Avionics validation
- Propulsion testing (including RDRE)
- GNC systems validation
- Reentry systems
- Hypersonic technologies

**Core value:** Reduce program risk · Compress timelines · Cut cost of failure

**Market context:** Wind tunnels are saturated. Modeling has limits. Flight truth is the constraint. EXOS solves that.

**EXOS is NOT a launch company.**
