WHO WE ARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exos Aerospace is one of only three FAA-licensed reusable rocket launch
providers in the United States, and the commercial successor to Armadillo
Aerospace, carrying more than fifteen years of vertical-takeoff / vertical-
landing (VTVL) rocket flight-test lineage.

Exos has flown commercial reusable suborbital rockets since 2018, operating its
SARGE vehicle across multiple flights with vehicle recovery and reflight. Our
current vehicle, the BLK3 Precision Suborbital Reusable Launch Vehicle (PSRLV),
received its FAA launch license on March 13, 2026, and is manifested for the
first commercial flight of BLK3 in early 2027. BLK3 is a VTVL, same-pad
reusable rocket designed for rapid-turnaround payload flight and recovery.

This makes Exos the only FAA-licensed suborbital reusable rocket provider
booking payloads to fly (early 2027) in a true rocket-boosted profile that is
distinct from the balloon, crewed-capsule, air-launched spaceplane, and orbital
providers already named in the FO4 notice.


FIT TO THE FO4 FLIGHT PROFILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FO4 procures payload integration and flight test services across six flight
profiles that give science and technology payloads access to relevant
environments for technology maturation. Exos delivers the suborbital reusable
rocket profile:

- Boosted ascent to a near-space / space suborbital apogee
- Sustained reduced-gravity (microgravity) coast phase
- Ascent and reentry thermal, acoustic, and vibration environments
- Powered VTVL descent and same-pad recovery of the vehicle and payload
- Rapid re-flight of the same airframe for iterative test-fly-fix campaigns

Exos supports the full integration life cycle NASA/AFRC requires: payload
accommodation and interface definition, integration and environmental
readiness, range and safety coordination under our FAA license, flight
operations, data delivery, and payload recovery and return.

DIFFERENTIATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Payload recovery and re-flight: powered VTVL landing returns the payload for
  post-flight inspection, enabling test-fly-fix cycles most FO profiles cannot.
- Minimal launch infrastructure: Exos requires only a pad with power and comms;
  a mobile command module brings the rest, and the vehicle launches and lands
  on the same pad. This lowers cost and expands where FO payloads can fly.
- Integrated Pressurized Structures (IPS): the propellant tank is integrated as
  primary load-bearing structure, reducing vehicle mass and cost.
- Flight-grade navigation on hand: two new, unused, dual-GNSS Advanced
  Navigation GEO-FOG 3D Dual FOG inertial navigation systems in inventory.
- Small business, commercial (FAR Part 12) provider with an active FAA license
  and established range-safety operations.
