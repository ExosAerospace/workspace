🛰️ AI Mission Desk

An agent-run pipeline that takes a payload / flight customer from inbound lead → signed contract with no human in the loop until the close. It targets Exos's most human-bottlenecked dimension — the customer-facing scope→quote→contract flow — and runs it on the AI stack we already operate. Goal: cut time-from-inbound-to-quote from days to minutes and run 10× the BD volume with the same team.
