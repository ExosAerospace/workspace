What Mission Control is

Think of it as one shared online board for everything we are working on: who is doing what, what is due, what is waiting on a decision, and where the documents live. Instead of work scattered across email, Teamwork, and people's heads, it all lives in one place everyone can see. You open it in a web browser. It is replacing Teamwork.

⚠️ Compliance — no CUI or ITAR data

At this time, do not attach documents to Mission Control, and do not BCC emails into it, that contain CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information) or ITAR-controlled data. That content lives only in its designated SharePoint folder. When in doubt, leave it out and keep it in SharePoint.

Step 3 — How to use it (the basics)

  • Your board: tasks are arranged in columns by status, for example To Do, In Progress, Done. The ones assigned to you are yours to move along.
  • Open a task: click any task to see its full details — description, due date, and notes.
  • Update a task: drag it from one column to the next as you make progress (or use the quick-move buttons), and add a note in the detail panel. That is how you "report status" — no separate update needed.
  • Add a task: use the "+ Add" bar at the top.
  • Decisions tab: anything that needs your review or sign-off shows up here. Answer it and the work moves forward.
  • Documents: attach a file directly to a task, so it lives with the work instead of buried in email.

Step 4 — The time-saver: let your email update your tasks for you

You do not have to log things by hand. On your normal work emails, just add exosmc@proton.me as a BCC. Mission Control reads who you are and what the email is about and automatically files it under the right task. No task numbers, no codes, nothing to look up — just add that one BCC and your everyday email becomes the running record.

Ask Exobot to do it — just @Exobot

Need something done? Type @Exobot and then, in plain English, the task. That is the entire trigger — everything you write after @Exobot is the instruction it carries out. No commands, no codes, no format.

  • Examples: @Exobot add a task to follow up with Marlett on Friday · @Exobot pull the latest on the AEDC solicitation and summarize it · @Exobot draft a reply to this email for my review.
  • It works the same way in chat and in email (put @Exobot plus your instruction at the top of a forwarded email).
  • The guardrail: Exobot does safe, internal work directly — creating and updating tasks, research, drafts, summaries, reminders. Anything external or sensitive — sending emails, government or contract submissions, financial or legal actions — it prepares and routes to Jon for approval first. It never sends those on its own.

Step 5 — NEW: turn a call into updates automatically (Transcripts)

Finished a call and have a transcript or notes? Feed it in and Mission Control does the write-up for you. Email the transcript with [TRANSCRIPT] in the subject line (attach the Word/text file, or paste it in the body). It will:

  • read who was on the call and add them to Contacts,
  • pull out the key points, decisions, and action items,
  • file it under the right relationship and propose an updated status and next step.

You get a short summary back and confirm before anything changes the record — so a mis-heard line never silently rewrites a deal. The transcript itself is archived for reference.

⚠️ Same compliance rule: do not submit transcripts of CUI or ITAR conversations (e.g., government technical calls). Keep those in SharePoint.

Relationship Dossiers — every relationship at a glance

The 🤝 Dossiers tab gives one card per key relationship (investors, partners, advisors, agencies): the contacts, the live deals, signed agreements/NDAs, the last time you talked, the current status, and the next step — all in one place. Last-touch and agreement status update themselves from email and the document archive.

Contacts — built automatically from your email

The 👥 Contacts tab captures everyone you correspond with, pulled automatically from email — name, company, last contact, and how many times you have exchanged messages. A flag shows whether they are already in the investor CRM, so nobody slips through the cracks. Use the "untracked only" toggle to see who is new. It grows on its own as you make contacts; nothing to enter by hand.

Your daily habit

Open Mission Control, glance at your tasks, move anything that progressed, check the Decisions tab, and BCC exosmc@proton.me on client emails. That is it.
