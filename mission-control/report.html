🛡️ Ethics & Compliance Report

Handbook Section 2.5 — report fraud, waste, abuse, safety, export-control, or any violation of law or policy. Retaliation is prohibited.
← Mission Control

File a report

You can report here, to any manager, to HR, to the CEO, or directly to the government (below). You never have to report internally first.
⚠ Do not enter CUI, ITAR/EAR technical data, or classified information. Describe it in general terms, or report it through an Inspector General hotline.

Check on a report / add information

Enter the case key you received when you filed the report.

Report directly to the government

DoD Hotline: 1-800-424-9098 · dodig.mil/hotline
NASA OIG Hotline: 1-800-424-9183 · oig.nasa.gov/hotline
You may also go to a Member of Congress, GAO, the Contracting Officer, DOJ/law enforcement, the SEC, EEOC, OSHA, or NLRB. Protected under 10 U.S.C. § 4701 and 41 U.S.C. § 4712. You can file a reprisal complaint with the agency Inspector General within 3 years. Government hotlines are the fully anonymous option. Mission Control requires a sign-in, even though anonymous reports here store no identity.
