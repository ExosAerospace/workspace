LVES Charter Working Group — Round 1 Survey

This short survey shapes the Launch Vehicles & Entry Systems (LVES) Technical Committee charter. It takes about 10 minutes and is mostly ranking and multi-select. Round 1 collects the full set of scope candidates and desired outcomes; Round 2 hands back a consolidated list for you to prioritize, then a draft charter comes to the next meeting. Where a question offers an anonymous option, feel free to use it so we get candid input on boundaries. Thank you for helping keep this charter sharp rather than shelf-bound.
