About Exos

The suborbital test platform that de-risks your orbital program

Exos Aerospace operates the SARGE reusable launch vehicle — a flight-proven suborbital platform that gives payload developers, government agencies, and commercial partners a fast, affordable path to space-relevant data.

We don't just build rockets. We fly them, recover them, and fly them again — delivering the iterative test cadence that orbital programs demand before committing to full-scale development.
